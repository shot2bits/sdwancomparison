# Harry’s buyer checklist fact check

Checked: 2026-11-10

Material factual premises in all 30 original buyer checklists, including product lifecycle, service ownership, assurance scope and commercial benchmarks. This is not a line-by-line validation of every imported profile table, a financial audit, or supplier confirmation of a buyer design.

Original wording is retained for provenance and may contain outdated premises. Read the current checks first. Unverified claims are not established facts; absence of a public document is not proof that a capability or certificate does not exist. These notes do not upgrade capability grades or certify buyer compliance.
