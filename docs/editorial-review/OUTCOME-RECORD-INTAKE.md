# First genuine Netify outcome records

Use the existing private project outcome workflow. Do not create synthetic customer records or bypass exact-version buyer approval.

For each candidate case, staff need:
- The existing private project and actual supplier approach/response records.
- The initial problem and site/user bands.
- What options the buyer initially considered, what Netify discovered and what changed in the shortlist or scope.
- Which suppliers responded or declined, and the evidenced timing.
- The decision or current status, including incomplete or abandoned sourcing.
- Underlying private evidence for every outcome assertion.

Write the public explanation without company names, staff identities, addresses, prices, proposal extracts or other identifying details. The current publication contract uses anonymous supplier counts; do not add names without a separately reviewed consent model.

Process: create a draft from the real project; staff review evidence and anonymity; obtain the verified buyer’s approval of that exact version; publish; verify the public feed. An edited version needs new permission. A withdrawn record must disappear from the public page and feed.
