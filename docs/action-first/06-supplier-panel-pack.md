# Response panel pilot pack — 30 September 2026

## One-page commercial discussion draft (not executed terms)

Netify introduces a buyer-approved opportunity to the named supplier. The supplier acknowledges the introduction before receiving the brief. The introduction is limited to the buyer, scope and opportunity reference recorded below; pre-existing relationships and overlapping opportunities are disclosed before acceptance. No exclusivity, commission amount or protection period is assumed.

The supplier names a pre-sales owner and confirms a response commitment in working days. The response is a written proposal covering the agreed scope, or a decline stating the reason. Missing costs, assumptions, validity, installation and recurring charges, delivery dependencies and tax/currency must be explicit. The supplier does not receive buyer contact details unless separately approved. No onward sharing beyond the approved delivery team. Netify coordinates clarification and records response timings. The buyer retains the decision; no contract award or minimum volume is promised.

Commission: schedule to be agreed between Netify and this supplier, including rate/basis, payment trigger, attribution evidence, pre-existing opportunity handling, disputes, cancellation and expiry. No enforceable commission protection is claimed from this draft alone. The relevant supplier arrangement must be agreed before disclosure.

Publication: private response data is never automatically published. Naming participants or publishing an anonymised outcome requires the specified separate consents. Research order is independent of commercial participation. Panel status may be withdrawn if the named contact or commitment expires.
